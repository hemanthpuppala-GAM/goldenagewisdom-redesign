You are an electromagnetic broadcast

The heart generates a magnetic field ~100× stronger than the brain's — measurable up to 15 feet around the body. What you feel, you transmit.

Static noise · stress cortisol · adrenaline

Fear and hurry make the heart rhythm jagged and the brain scattered — an incoherent signal that broadcasts, and attracts, more static.

Coherence · meditation oxytocin · DHEA

A quiet heart broadcasts a smooth, organised wave. Like a tuning fork, a coherent field sets the space around it humming in sympathy.

◉ The observer

Attention collapses possibility into experience — what you steadily watch, you make real.

∞ Entanglement

Your field is never isolated — influence travels between hearts without physical contact.

♒ Resonance

The Coherence Protocol · daily
01 · Morning coherence

Before opening your eyes: 10 minutes of slow 5-second rhythmic breathing — set the day's frequency first.

02 · Scan & interrupt

Catch static in real time. On tension, breathe 5-5-7 — the longer exhale calms the nervous system out of survival mode.

03 · Feel it done

The field responds to feeling, not thought — gratitude in advance broadcasts the signal of a life already fulfilled.
